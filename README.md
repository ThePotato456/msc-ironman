## About

Iron Man Suit adds a slow, hands-on build to My Summer Car. Nothing is handed to you. Parts come by mail order from the
Teho-Tarvike catalogue in your mailbox, the palladium is scrap from the landfill, and the work is done with the game's
own spanners, soldering iron and hammer. First you build the arc reactor at the workbench chuck. Then you build a suit
piece by piece on a hoist in your garage, drop the reactor into the chest, flip the switch and step in.

Two suits are included: the rough **cave Mk1** from the cave in Afghanistan, and the red and gold **Mk3**.

## Features

- **A mail-order catalogue.** A Teho-Tarvike catalogue waits in your mailbox, in Finnish like the rest of the game. Fill
  in the order form, post it, and the parcel arrives at Teimo's counter a day or two later.
- **A buildable arc reactor.** Fit the cage, core and palladium, bolt the core down, solder six joints, charge it, wind ten
  copper coils, bolt the ring, and light it. The light-guide ring glows blue. A bad solder joint or an empty core flickers
  and fails.
- **Salvage.** The palladium comes from a scrap catalytic converter at the landfill.
- **Two full suits.** The cave Mk1 (helmet, chest, shoulders, forearms, thighs and boots) and the red and gold Mk3 with
  its own pieces, a diffuser plate in the chest and a helmet with a faceplate that opens.
- **A suit hoist.** It sets up in your garage with your first suit order. Each piece is hung, bolted to the jig, soldered
  and plated by hand with sheet steel from the landfill and hammered to shape.
- **A power-up.** Fit the finished reactor in the chest, flip the switch on the hoist's leg, and the suit lights up and
  steps off the jig.
- **Wear it and fly it.** Get into the standing suit and fly with a boost, a palm repulsor and a faceplate that lifts. A
  HUD shows power, altitude and speed.
- **A battery to manage.** A full reactor flies about 15 minutes. Recharge by standing the empty suit next to the charger.
- **Pieces you can test-wear** before the suit is finished, and take off again. A finished piece keeps its progress when
  taken off the hoist.
- **Helmets you can pick up.** The Mk3 helmet stands on the workbench with eyes that switch on and a faceplate that opens.
  The Mk1 helmet displays the same way.
- **It remembers.** Parts, bolts, solder, plating, the hoist, the suit's charge and what you are wearing are kept in the
  game's own save.

## Requirements

- **My Summer Car** on Windows.
- **[MSCLoader](https://github.com/piotrulos/MSCModLoader)**, installed into the game.

## Installation

1. Install [MSCLoader](https://github.com/piotrulos/MSCModLoader) into My Summer Car, if you haven't already.
2. Unzip the download into the game's `Mods` folder. It holds `IronManSuit.dll` and an `Assets/IronManSuit/` folder,
   which end up as `Mods/IronManSuit.dll` and `Mods/Assets/IronManSuit/`.
3. Start the game and load a save. Look for the catalogue in your mailbox.

The mod adds itself to an existing save. It needs no new game. Remember that the mod's meshes are loaded from the assets
folder: if it is missing, parts will not appear.

## How to play

### The arc reactor

1. **Order.** Look at the catalogue in your mailbox and press **Use** to read it. Click a product to add one to the
   order form, right-click to take one off, then press **TILAA NYT**. Page 1 sells the reactor parts:

   | Part | Price |
   |---|---|
   | Cage | 650 MK |
   | Core housing | 1,500 MK |
   | Light-guide ring | 950 MK |
   | Copper wire | 500 MK |

   Delivery is 85 MK. Your first order also brings a battery charger and a soldering iron, free.
2. **Post and pick up.** Press Use at your mailbox to post the form. A day or two later the phone rings and the parcel is
   waiting on Teimo's counter. Click it to pay, carry it home and press Use on it to open it.
3. **Salvage.** The palladium comes from a scrap catalytic converter at the landfill. Undo its two flange bolts.
4. **Build at the workbench chuck:**
   1. Put the cage in and tighten the jaws.
   2. Fit the core and the palladium.
   3. Bolt the core down: 3 x 8 mm.
   4. Solder its 6 joints: hold Use with the iron.
   5. Charge it with the charger's clamps.
   6. Fit the ring and wind its 10 coils from the wire spool.
   7. Bolt the ring: 3 x 7 mm.
   8. First light.

A finished reactor can sit on its stand, or go into a suit's chest.

### The suits

Turn the catalogue's page with the corner tab or the arrow keys. Page 2 is the cave Mk1 and page 3 is the Mk3. Both come
as six frames: helmet, chest, shoulders, forearms, thighs and boots. Pairs come as a left and right.

| Mk3 piece | Price |
|---|---|
| Helmet | 3,200 MK |
| Chest (with its diffuser plate) | 5,600 MK |
| Shoulders, pair | 3,600 MK |
| Forearms, pair | 4,400 MK |
| Thighs, pair | 3,000 MK |
| Boots, pair | 4,600 MK |

Your first suit order brings the suit hoist free. Opening the parcel sets it up in the garage, and sheets of scrap steel
appear at the landfill. A Mk3 owner needs ten of them.

### Building a suit on the hoist

For each piece:

1. Hold the frame at the hoist and left-click to hang it.
2. Bolt it to the jig with your spanners.
3. Solder its joints with the iron (hold Use).
4. Lay a sheet of steel on it (left-click), then hammer it to shape: empty hand, hold Use. The Mk1's forearms take no
   plate.

With every bolt undone, right-click takes a piece off. It keeps its progress.

To try a finished piece, hold it and press **Use** to test-wear it. **Z** takes them all off.

### Powering up

Left-click the finished arc reactor into the chest socket, then flip the switch on the hoist's leg. The suit lights up
and steps off the jig. A suit that has been powered up stands in the garage until you take it out.

### Wearing and flying

Press **Use** at the standing suit to get in. Inside:

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **V** | Fly or land. Steer with the move keys toward where you look. |
| **Space** | Climb. |
| **C** | Descend. |
| **Shift** | Boost. |
| **R** | Palm repulsor. |
| **Y** | Faceplate up or down. |
| **G** | Get out. |

The HUD shows power, altitude, speed and whether the repulsor is ready.

### Charging

A full reactor flies about 15 minutes. To recharge, stand the empty suit next to the charger and switch the charger on.

### The helmets on display

The Mk3 helmet is set once per save on a clear patch of the garage workbench, resting on its neck. Carry it like any
part. **Right-click** switches its eyes on and off, and **Use** on the release disc at either side lifts its faceplate.
The Mk1 helmet displays and lights the same way.

### Saving

The mod stores its state in the game's own save, so it follows your save slot: ordered and delivered parts, bolts, solder
and plating, what hangs on the hoist, which suit stands in the garage, its charge, and what you wear.

My Summer Car's saves are in `%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Amistech\My Summer Car`. Back them up before you try
anything risky: with permadeath on, a death deletes the save.

## Settings and keys

The suit's keys can be changed in the mod's settings, under **Suit**:

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Fly / land (in the suit) | V |
| Repulsor (in the suit) | R |
| Get out of the suit | G |
| Faceplate up / down | Y |
| Take off worn test pieces | Z |

## Known issues

- **Mk1 and Mk3 are meshes built by hand.** They follow the reference images closely but are still being refined, so
  expect rough edges.
- **No second reactor in one suit.** A suit takes one reactor, and a reactor that is in a suit cannot be used elsewhere.
- **The Mk2 is not included.** It is nearly the same suit as the Mk3.
- **Finnish text.** The catalogue and the order form are in Finnish, like the game's own shops.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No catalogue in the mailbox | Check that MSCLoader is installed and that `IronManSuit.dll` is in the game's `Mods` folder. |
| No parts, sounds or textures | The files in `Mods/Assets/IronManSuit/` are missing or in the wrong folder. Copy them again. |
| The parcel doesn't arrive | Post the order form at the mailbox with Use. It arrives a day or two later and the phone rings. |
| The suit won't power up | The chest needs a finished reactor, and every piece must be bolted, soldered and plated. |
| The suit won't fly | Check the HUD's power. An empty reactor needs a charge. |
| The player lands at the Peräjärvi churchyard | The landing spot was under the terrain. Climb and try again. |
| Something else is wrong | The game writes a log to `output_log.txt` in its folder. Attach it to your report. |

## Uninstalling

Delete `IronManSuit.dll` from the `Mods` folder, and the `Mods/Assets/IronManSuit` folder. Your save still loads without
the mod.

## Reporting problems

Open an [issue](https://github.com/glizzy5oblin/msc-ironman/issues) and say what you did, which suit you were building,
and what happened. Add `output_log.txt` from the game folder if you can.

## Building from source

You need the .NET SDK, My Summer Car and MSCLoader. `make setup` checks them, `make build` compiles to
`src/bin/Release/net35/IronManSuit.dll`, and `make build DEPLOY=1` also copies the dll and assets into the game's `Mods`
folder. Pass `GAME="D:/Games/My Summer Car"` if the game is not in the default Steam folder.
